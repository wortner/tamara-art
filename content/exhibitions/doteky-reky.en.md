**Works on handmade paper, 2024–2026**

The work of České Budějovice-based therapist and artist Tamara Wortnerová bridges two seemingly incompatible worlds — the world of art and of physiotherapy. Looking closer, though, these two worlds are much closer to one another than they might at first appear.

In her therapeutic practice, Tamara Wortnerová meets the body's stories every day, listens to her clients, and the experience of these encounters is later carried into her paintings. Exploring the subtle layers of the body and nervous system led her, some years ago, to work with the system of water in the body — the so-called fluid body — and so the theme of water organically "flowed over" into her artistic practice too.

Tamara Wortnerová experiences fluidity in the body as a life-giving principle, one that, with sensitive attunement, we can feel, sense the presence of, and communicate with. It is precisely this lived experience that she seeks to communicate through her paintings. She wants to show everything the river of life carries, how life is contained in every drop of water.

Yet this is not mere thematic inspiration or outward record. For the artist, her paintings and drawings form a kind of notation, intuitive protocols processing dives into the waters and rivers of the body. The experiences held within them run deep, yet in the therapeutic work they are entirely concrete, even as they take on abstract form on the canvas. The surfaces of her paintings are gradually filled not only with pencil drawing but also acrylic and watercolor; in further layers she adds found natural materials — shells, feathers — as well as embroidery and lace, in an effort to describe, to map, to touch the space as fully as possible.

"Water always finds its way, its principle is to flow, to live... it is immortal like the human soul," says Tamara. "Attention to the presence of water in the body, that connection to the miraculous river of life, heals, connects, unifies. It is water that connects everything to everything, holding within it the deep calm and the wildness of the ocean, the murmur of small streams, and the microworld of a single drop briefly closed in on itself. In water, as in the soul, there is immense wisdom... I paint the imprints of this communication, sometimes successfully, sometimes not, but I hope always sincerely."

Tamara Wortnerová's paintings are an imprint of water and intuition in color and paper, a mirroring of the microcosm and the universe, drops of experience from which an entire river can arise.
